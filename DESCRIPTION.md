**Nullgate's Totems** is a shaman addon for **World of Warcraft: Forever**. Four element buttons drop the totems you choose at your highest rank, with timers, a party range list and a weapon imbue reminder.

Every totem duration comes straight from the Forever client, where most totems last 5 minutes, so the timers match the game you're playing rather than original Classic.

> **Beta:** this is an early test version and we're looking for shamans to try it. If something doesn't work, run `/ntotem check`, then send us the results with a short description.

---

## Features

- **Earth, Fire, Water and Air buttons**: each drops the totem you chose for that element at the highest rank you know. Right-click or hover a button to choose a different totem.
- **Drop All**: place your four chosen totems, one press per totem.
- **Totem timers** on each button, with warnings when a totem is about to expire, is destroyed early, or you've moved out of its range.
- **Party range list**: for totems that buff your party, see who has the buff and who is too far away.
- **Weapon imbue button and reminder**: one click casts your chosen imbue (Rockbiter, Flametongue, Frostbrand or Windfury Weapon) at your highest rank, with a reminder when it's missing or running low.
- **Totem sets**: save sets such as "Raid melee", "Caster group" or "Fire resist" and switch all four choices at once.
- **Key bindings** for every button, under Key Bindings > AddOns.
- **Simple settings**: lock and move the bar, vertical or horizontal layout, scale, warnings and the language.

---

## How It Works

1. **Choose a totem for each element.**
2. **Click a button**, or press its key binding, to drop that totem. Use Drop All to place all four.
3. **Watch the timers.** The addon warns you before a totem runs out.

The game doesn't allow buttons to change during combat. If you choose a different totem mid-fight, the button shows a small red mark and switches as soon as combat ends.

## Slash Commands

| Command | Action |
| :--- | :--- |
| `/ntotem` or `/totems` | Open the settings. |
| `/ntotem save <name>` | Save your four totem choices as a set. |
| `/ntotem set <name>` | Switch to a saved totem set. |
| `/ntotem sets` / `/ntotem delete <name>` | List your sets, or delete one. |
| `/ntotem lock` / `/ntotem unlock` | Lock or unlock the bar. |
| `/ntotem reset` | Reset the bar's position. |
| `/ntotem check` | Test what the addon needs in your game client and save the results (for bug reports). |

---

## Installation

1. Download the latest file.
2. Extract the `NullgatesTotems-Forever` folder into the `Interface\AddOns` folder of your World of Warcraft: Forever installation.
3. Log in on a shaman. The bar appears near the bottom of the screen; type `/ntotem` for the settings.

---

## Good to Know

- The addon only runs for shamans.
- Totem durations and ranks are read from the Forever client data, not copied from original Classic.
- Available in 10 languages: English, Deutsch, Español, Français, Italiano, Português (Brasil), Русский, 한국어, 简体中文 and 繁體中文 (every language the game client runs in). The game's language is used automatically, and you can pick another in the settings. The translations were made with machine assistance; corrections are welcome.
